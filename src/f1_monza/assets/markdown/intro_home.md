# F1 _Group 2

Monza isn't a circuit you tame. It's one you survive — three years of data, three different
stories. From Sainz's Ferrari pole heartbreak in 2023 to Norris's commanding 2025 victory,
the **Italian Grand Prix** rewards aerodynamic gambles, mechanical confidence, and pit-wall
bravery in equal measure.

This dashboard is the **pit-stops view** into those three races (2023–2025). Open the
**Tire Strategy** page to see who gambled on a one-stopper, the **Laps** page for a sector
breakdown of the Temple of Speed, and **Weather** for the conditions that shaped each call.
